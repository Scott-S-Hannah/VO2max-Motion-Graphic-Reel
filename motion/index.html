What is V̇O₂max?

Oxygen as light. A real 3D camera with depth of field flies through thousands of particles. The type is lit by the scene. Each act has its own colour, and everything is rendered live on the GPU and cut to the soundtrack. Press play with sound on, scrub, or jump to a beat.

    Everything is a pure function of time, so any frame can be rendered exactly: reel.seek(t), or reel.renderBlur(t) for export with sub-frame motion blur, at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Live quality adapts to the device.
